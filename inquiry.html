How we work

Curated procurement

For clients seeking an existing work, we source and present pieces that suit the room, the collection, or the qualities sought. When a request merits a more exacting search, selection may extend to in-person sourcing in China.

Bespoke commission

For clients seeking something made to vision, we work with artists and makers to shape the piece around form, dimensions, glaze, or set. The process is guided privately from concept through final delivery.
