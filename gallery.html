Representative Works and Categories

These categories reflect the materials, aesthetics, and sourcing interests that inform our practice. This page is intended as a visual portfolio rather than a traditional shop.

Redware

Bold forms and saturated glaze treatments suited to both display and contemporary interiors.

Mongolian Silver

Distinctive silverwork and vessels with strong material presence and regional specificity.

Quietly refined pieces emphasizing tone, restraint, and harmony of form.
