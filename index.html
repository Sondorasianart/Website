Refined Procurement

A Refined Approach to Art Procurement

We help clients source remarkable works that are difficult to access through typical North American retail channels. From contemporary Jianware and redware to blue-and-white porcelain and Mongolian silver, Sondor Asian Art combines scholarly insight, direct artisan relationships, and concierge-level service to guide each acquisition from concept to delivery.

Services

What We Do

Curated Procurement

For clients seeking a finished work, we identify and source pieces that align with a specific aesthetic, category, or spatial vision. We manage shortlisting, vetting, coordination, and delivery so the process is clear and efficient.

Bespoke Commissions

For projects that require something custom, we work with artisans to commission pieces tailored to your needs, including form, glaze, scale, inscription, and matching sets.

Partnership & Placement

We collaborate with select design, gallery, hospitality, and retail partners to place works in meaningful spaces and create opportunities for curated displays, referrals, and special projects.
