About

Sondor Asian Art presents ceramics and silver chosen for presence, proportion, and material depth.

Elizabeth Rose

Elizabeth Frances Rose is a PhD candidate at the Centre for Medieval Studies at the University of Toronto. A Chancellor’s Scholar at Queen’s University, she holds master’s degrees in art history and medieval studies from the University of Toronto.

Elizabeth's research encompasses the artistic cultures of Inner and East Asia, medieval Europe, and the Islamic world, with a focus on the art of the Mongol Empire and its successor states.

Elizabeth is currently working on a dissertation tentatively entitled “Mobile Arts of the Mongol Empires,” which examines Mongol art objects, such as tents, belts, and saddles, from the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries.

In the pursuit of her research into premodern Eurasian art history, Elizabeth has spent extensive time conducting fieldwork in China and Mongolia. She is fluent in Modern Standard Chinese and Mongolian, among other languages.
