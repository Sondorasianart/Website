About Sondor Asian Art

Sondor Asian Art is a Toronto-based art procurement studio specializing in East Asian ceramics, silverwork, and bespoke decorative objects.

We serve collectors, interior designers, and hospitality clients seeking distinctive works with greater depth, authenticity, and character than what is typically available through standard retail channels.

Our approach is shaped by direct artisan relationships, scholarly knowledge, and a strong understanding of client service. We focus on helping clients acquire exceptional pieces with confidence, whether that means locating an existing work or managing a made-to-spec commission from concept through completion.

Our Perspective

We believe that meaningful objects carry more than aesthetic value. They hold technical skill, regional identity, cultural memory, and the touch of the maker. Sondor exists to connect North American clients with these works through a process that is transparent, tailored, and respectful of both the client’s vision and the artist’s craft.

Who We Work With

We work with private collectors, interior designers, and selected hospitality and retail partners. Some clients come to us with a precise request. Others come with a feeling, a palette, a room, or a story they want a piece to express. In both cases, we guide the sourcing process with care and discretion.

Founders

Elizabeth “Ellie” Rose

Ellie is Co-Founder of Sondor Asian Art. Her academic work in Mongolian art history and research in China have informed a deep understanding of regional artistic traditions and helped her build relationships with artisans and makers.

Carter Smith

Carter is Co-Founder of Sondor Asian Art. With a background in finance and business operations, he focuses on client development, partnerships, and the coordination needed to bring each project from inquiry to completion.
