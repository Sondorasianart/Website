About

Sondor Asian Art presents ceramics and silver chosen for presence, proportion, and material depth.

Approach

The emphasis is on objects that hold their place quietly: ceramics with weight and surface, silver with clarity and reserve.

Perspective

Selections are made with collectors, interiors, and long view in mind. Works are offered sparingly and by inquiry.
