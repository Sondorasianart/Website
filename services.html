Services

Tailored Procurement, Not Traditional Inventory

Sondor Asian Art responds to client needs by sourcing existing works or coordinating custom commissions through trusted artisan relationships.

Curated Procurement

This service is designed for clients seeking an existing piece that fits a particular aesthetic, function, or environment. We source works based on your vision, shortlist suitable options, and manage the process through selection, vetting, logistics, and delivery coordination.

Bespoke Commission

When a piece does not yet exist, we can coordinate a custom commission with the appropriate artisan. This may involve specific dimensions, glaze preferences, inscriptions, formal references, or a matching set created for a residential, hospitality, or collector context.

Partnership & Placement

We work selectively with partners in design, hospitality, gallery, and retail environments to create opportunities for curated displays, referrals, and special placements in thoughtful, high-credibility settings.

Process

How It Works

  1. Inquiry. Tell us what you are looking for, including category, style, dimensions, timeline, and visual references.
  2. Review. We assess whether the request is best fulfilled through sourcing an existing work or commissioning a new one.
  3. Sourcing or Commission. We begin identifying options or coordinating with an artisan, depending on the nature of the request.
  4. Selection and Approval. We present the most suitable direction, refine details where needed, and confirm the path forward.
  5. Coordination and Delivery. We oversee the process through completion and coordinate shipping and delivery.

Because each request is unique, all projects begin with an inquiry.
